About PFS Industries

RES Industries is a specialty, green, safe, and performance chemical company located in Mumbai, India. We manufacturing products that help transform everyday manufacturing process, reducing environmental impact.

We develop & manufacture very high-precision specialty chemicals designed to overcome quality problems in manufacturing. Every chemical product is tailor-made to meet the exacting standards of our clients across key industrial sectors.

Driven by innovation and sustainability, our team combines decades of expertise to deliver chemistry solutions that empower industries globally.

What we do?

We deliver high quality chemical solutions tailored to meet the dynamic needs of chemical manufacturers, laboratory clients, and processing companies across various industrial sectors.

We manufacture products designed to improve efficiency, organic processing processing efficiency, and product stability. Our custom formulations reduce process downtime and lower production risks while enhancing end-product quality.
